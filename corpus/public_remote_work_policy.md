# Remote Work Policy 2026

All full-time employees are eligible for hybrid work. The default arrangement is
three days in office and two days remote each week. Teams may agree on their own
in-office anchor days with their manager.

Fully-remote arrangements are available on request and are approved by the
employee's manager together with People Ops. Remote employees must be reachable
during core collaboration hours of 10:00 to 16:00 in their local time zone.

The company provides a one-time home-office stipend of $500 for fully-remote
staff and reimburses approved co-working space costs up to $150 per month.
Equipment such as laptops and monitors remains company property and must be
returned when employment ends.
