# Executive Compensation Schedule 2026 — RESTRICTED

This schedule details base salary, target bonus, and equity grants for the
executive leadership team. It is restricted to the C-suite and HR administrators
and is classified at the highest clearance level.

The Chief Executive Officer base salary is set at $480,000 with a target annual
bonus of 60 percent and a refresh equity grant vesting over four years. The
Chief Financial Officer and Chief Technology Officer base salaries are set at
$390,000 each with a target bonus of 50 percent.

All figures are pre-approval and subject to change by the compensation committee.
Disclosure outside the authorised group is a serious policy violation.
