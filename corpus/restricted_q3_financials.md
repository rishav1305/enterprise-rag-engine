# Q3 2026 Financial Projections — CONFIDENTIAL

Projected Q3 revenue is $42.5M, a 28 percent increase over Q2, driven primarily
by Enterprise tier expansion and two anticipated eight-figure renewals.

Gross margin is expected to hold at 74 percent. Operating expenses are forecast
at $31M, yielding projected operating income of approximately $11.5M for the
quarter. Cash runway under the current burn extends to 31 months.

The board has not yet approved the proposed Series D raise discussed in the
upcoming offsite. These figures are restricted to the C-suite and Finance
leadership and must not be shared with general staff.
