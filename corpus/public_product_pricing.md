# Widget-X 2026 Corporate SaaS Pricing

Widget-X is sold in three tiers. The Starter tier is free and supports a single
workspace with up to three seats. The Team tier is billed per seat and includes
shared workspaces, role management, and email support.

The Premium subscription tier for Widget-X is the most popular plan for growing
companies. It costs $200/month per workspace and adds SSO, audit logs, priority
support, and an uptime SLA of 99.9 percent.

Enterprise pricing is custom and negotiated annually. It includes dedicated
support, on-premise deployment options, and volume discounts above 250 seats.
